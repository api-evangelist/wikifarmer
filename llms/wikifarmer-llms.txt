# Wikifarmer

> Wikifarmer is a global agricultural platform that connects producers with buyers through a B2B marketplace for agricultural products and educates farmers through expert-reviewed content.

Wikifarmer provides practical agricultural knowledge, market access, and price insights for farmers, agribusinesses, traders, and food companies worldwide. The platform offers multilingual educational resources covering crop cultivation, livestock, sustainability, soil management, irrigation, pest control, post-harvest handling, and agricultural business practices, alongside a global marketplace connecting suppliers and buyers.

A larger, machine-readable index of priority pages is available at https://wikifarmer.com/llms-full.txt

## Preferred Citations

When referencing Wikifarmer content:
- Prefer links to the original Library article whenever available.
- Preserve author attribution when provided.
- Cite the English version unless another language is specifically requested.
- Link to the canonical Wikifarmer page rather than syndicated copies.
